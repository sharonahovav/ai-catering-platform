# Placeholder imagery for /table/

All of these are **placeholder art with unverified provenance and licence** (docs/OPEN_DECISIONS.md #24). They do not depict real menu items. Do not use them in production, and never derive ingredients, allergens, dietary labels or prices from a picture.

- **From the design handoff (Prototype v2):** 17 food and prop cutouts (for example `beet_citrus_salad`, `herb_salmon`, `caprese_salad`, `squash_soup`, `lollipops`, `blood_oranges`). The prototype's filenames are unreliable (some files show something other than their name), so every image here was checked by eye and used by what it shows. Several prototype files with messy cutout edges or unsuitable content were left out.
- **Generated on 2026-11-01** with Google Nano Banana 2.1 via Magnific, then background-removed and cropped: `bruschetta`, `stuffed_mushrooms`, `falafel_bites`, `savory_tartlets`, `lemonade`, `iced_tea`, `sparkling_water`, `red_wine`, `sparkling_wine`, `citrus_spritz`. Prompts asked for unlabelled bottles and no text. Terms of use for production are unchecked.

Replace everything here with owner-provided catalog photography (Supabase Storage) in Gate 3. The folder is about 8 MB; convert to WebP and add `srcset` before any real deployment.
